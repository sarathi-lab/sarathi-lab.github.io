Privacy Policy

Application: NMCLE Quiz App (com.windi.nmclequiz)
Last Updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NMCLE Quiz App ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application designed for medical licensing exam preparation.

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Device Data:

  • Quiz Progress & Bookmarks: Your quiz answers, scores, bookmark entries, and test records are saved locally on your device via the Android Room database for offline accessibility.
  • User Preferences: Text-to-Speech audio toggles, theme choices, and audio pace preferences are stored locally on your device.

B. Cloud Services (Google Firebase):

  • The app accesses Google Cloud Firestore to stream and synchronize verified NMCLE question banks, explanations, and categories.
  • No personal registration, social login, or password creation is required to use the application.

C. Advertisements & Technical Diagnostics:

  • Google AdMob: The application displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect and use device information (such as Google Advertising ID, approximate network state, and IP address) to serve non-intrusive advertisements in compliance with Google's policies.

2. Permissions Used

We request minimal permissions strictly needed for the core learning functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Needed to download question banks from Firestore and load advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to check network reachability to switch between online and cached offline mode.
  • TTS_SERVICE: Connects to your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech engine if you choose to listen to questions.

We do not access sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Files, or Precise Location.

3. Third-Party Services

The app relies on trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for medical students, healthcare practitioners, and examination candidates. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your quiz progress and answers are stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all stored app data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory guidelines. Any modifications will be posted with an updated date.
