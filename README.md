# Privacy Policy for NMCLE Quiz App

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **NMCLE Quiz App** ("we", "our", or "the App"), with package name `com.windi.nmclequiz`, collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
- **Quiz Performance & Progress:** The app saves your local quiz answers, high scores, bookmarks, and completed tests locally on your device using Android Room Database to provide you with offline progress tracking.
- **Preferences:** Your preferences (such as audio/TTS settings and dark mode toggle) are saved locally.

### B. Cloud Services & Databases (Firebase Firestore)
- The app uses **Google Cloud Firestore** to fetch medical question banks, explanations, categories, and test sets.
- We do **not** require user account registration, passwords, phone numbers, or social media logins to access the quizzes.
- Anonymous identifiers or diagnostic IDs generated by the Firebase SDK may be used strictly to facilitate database query delivery and secure API communication.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data & Advertising
- **AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK):** The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and network state) to serve advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
- **Analytics & Diagnostics:** Basic crash and operational telemetry may be collected by Google Play Services to ensure app stability and prevent crashes.

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to download question sets from Firestore, retrieve updates, and load AdMob advertisements.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to detect network connectivity status so the app knows whether to serve cached offline questions or fetch live updates.
- `android.intent.action.TTS_SERVICE`: Allows the app to utilize your device's native Text-to-Speech engine to read medical questions aloud if you choose.

We do **not** request sensitive runtime permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Precise GPS Location, Contacts, or Storage.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information collected solely to:
- Deliver, maintain, and enhance the NMCLE Quiz App experience.
- Provide subject-wise past question practice, mock tests, and explanations.
- Enable offline study capability.
- Display relevant advertisements to keep the app accessible to medical students.

## 6. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play & Apple Compliant)

We respect your right to control your personal data:
- **In-App Account Deletion:** Any user logged into an account can permanently delete their account and associated data directly within the app:
  1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the top right corner.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the profile dialog and tap **"Delete Account & Data"**.
  3. Confirm the permanent deletion prompt.
- **What is Deleted:**
  - Your user authentication record is permanently deleted from **Firebase Authentication**.
  - All user documents, bookmarks, and quiz progress stored in **Google Cloud Firestore** under `users/{uid}` and `usernames/{username}` are permanently wiped.
  - All locally stored user attempt history and cached data on the device (Room Database) are cleared.
- **Guest / Local Users:** Users practicing without an account can clear all locally stored data at any time via Android **Settings > Apps > NMCLE Quiz App > Storage > Clear Storage** or by uninstalling the application.
